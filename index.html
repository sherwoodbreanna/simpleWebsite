My Core Values

Values | Industry

Loyalty

Description: Loyalty means standing by the people and principles that matter to me, even when it is difficult or inconvenient.

Where it derives from: Loyalty for me is derived from knowing that someone has your back even when it is hard.

Why it is important: Loyalty is important because it builds trust and strengthens long-term relationships with others.

Ramifications: Without loyalty, trust is broken and relationships weaken over time. People may become hesitant to rely on someone who does not remain consistent or dependable.

Respect

Description: Respect is treating others with dignity, fairness, and consideration regardless of differences or disagreements.

Where it derives from: Respect for me, comes from identifying someones qualities and treating them fairly no matter what.

Why it is important: Respect is important because it creates positive interactions and allows people to work together effectively.

Ramifications: Failing to show respect often leads to conflict and damaged relationships. A lack of respect can create negative environments and make collaboration difficult.

Kindness

Description: Kindness is showing compassion, understanding, and generosity toward others in everyday actions.

Where it derives from: Most times a lot of misunderstandings can occur and can be avoided with just a little kindness.

Why it is important: Kindness is important because it fosters empathy and helps create supportive and welcoming environments.

Ramifications: Without kindness, interactions can become cold or hostile, pushing people away. This can harm relationships and create unnecessary tension in both personal and professional settings.

Courage

Description: Courage is the ability to stand up for what is right and take action even in the face of fear or uncertainty.

Where it derives from: This is one of my personal values because I had to be courageous when attending new schools and meeting new people.

Why it is important: Courage is important because it allows individuals to stand up for their beliefs and take meaningful action despite challenges.

Ramifications: A lack of courage can result in missed opportunities and regret. It may also allow harmful situations to continue because difficult decisions were avoided.
